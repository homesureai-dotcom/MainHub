# Vision

HomeSure AI is the Operating System for Property Ownership.

Our mission is to help every homeowner understand, protect, improve and manage their property through intelligent insights powered by the HomeSure Property Health Index™.

---

# North Star

Every homeowner should be able to answer four questions within 30 seconds of opening HomeSure.

1. How healthy is my property?
2. What should I do next?
3. What changed recently?
4. What should I worry about?

Every feature must contribute to answering one of these questions.
