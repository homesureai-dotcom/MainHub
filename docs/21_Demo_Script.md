# Opening

"Buying a home is probably the biggest investment most of us ever make.

Yet after taking possession, homeowners have no simple way to understand whether their property is legally compliant, operationally healthy, or gradually becoming a liability.

HomeSure AI changes that."

---

# The Problem

Today's homeowners struggle because:

- Property documents are scattered.
- Compliance status is unclear.
- Maintenance issues accumulate silently.
- Risks are discovered only after they become expensive.
- There is no single health score for a property.

Owning a home should not require becoming an expert in legal, engineering, electrical and compliance matters.

---

# Introducing HomeSure AI

HomeSure AI is the Operating System for Property Ownership.

It continuously helps homeowners:

- Understand
- Protect
- Improve
- Manage

their property's health.

# Live Demo

## Step 1

Open Dashboard.

Explain:

"This is the Property Command Center."

Show:

- Property Health
- Summary Cards
- Recommended Actions
- Timeline
- Risk Center

---

## Step 2

Open Assessment.

Explain:

"The assessment evaluates every important aspect of the property."

Answer a few sample questions.

Complete assessment.

---

## Step 3

Show Property Health Score.

Explain:

"This score is calculated across multiple categories including legal compliance, construction quality, safety, utilities and documentation."

Highlight:

- Overall Score
- Category Breakdown
- Strengths
- Weaknesses

---

## Step 4

Open Property Health Report.

Explain:

"This becomes a professional report homeowners can use to understand their property's current condition."

Highlight:

- Executive Summary
- Risks
- Recommendations
- Category Analysis

---

## Step 5

Return to Dashboard.

Show:

Recommended Actions.

Explain:

"HomeSure doesn't just identify problems—it tells you what to do next."

Example:

Upload Fire NOC

+8 Property Health

---

## Step 6

Show Property Timeline.

Explain:

"Every important event becomes part of the property's history."

Examples:

- e-Khata approved
- BESCOM connected
- Fire NOC renewed
- STP maintenance completed

---

## Step 7

Show Risk Center.

Explain:

"The Risk Center highlights issues before they become expensive problems."

Highlight:

- Critical Risks
- Medium Risks
- Low Risks

---

## Step 8

Open SureBot.

Ask:

"Why is my Property Health only 78?"

Explain:

"SureBot translates complex property information into plain language and recommends the highest-impact actions."
