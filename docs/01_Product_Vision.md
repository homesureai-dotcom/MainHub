# HomeSure AI

## Product Vision

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Vision Statement

HomeSure AI is an AI-powered Property Intelligence Platform that helps homeowners, apartment buyers, resident associations, builders, banks and property investors understand the health, compliance, documentation and long-term value of residential properties.

Our goal is to become the "Credit Score for Properties."

---

# Mission

To make property ownership transparent, measurable and trustworthy through AI-driven insights.

Instead of homeowners wondering:

- Is my property legally safe?
- Is my builder trustworthy?
- Which documents are missing?
- Will this affect resale value?

HomeSure AI provides a single Property Health Score along with personalized recommendations and AI-powered guidance.

---

# Problem Statement

Buying or owning a property involves hundreds of documents, approvals and compliance requirements.

Today homeowners face problems such as:

- Missing Occupancy Certificates
- Missing Fire NOC
- Missing eKhata
- BWSSB/BESCOM compliance issues
- Poor builder transparency
- Lack of centralized property documentation
- No objective way to measure property health

Information is scattered across multiple agencies, builders and documents.

There is no single platform that provides a complete health assessment of a property.

---

# Solution

HomeSure AI creates a digital health record for every property.

The platform combines:

- Property Health Assessment
- AI-powered Recommendations
- Builder Intelligence
- Document Vault
- Compliance Tracking
- SureBot AI Assistant
- Property Health Reports
- PDF Reports
- Property Portfolio Management
