# HomeSure Property Health Index™ (PHI™) Standard

Version: 1.0

Last Updated: August 2026

---

# Purpose

The HomeSure Property Health Standard™ (HPHS) defines the methodology used to evaluate the overall health, compliance, safety and readiness of a residential property.

It provides a consistent framework for calculating the HomeSure Property Health Score™ and generating personalized recommendations.

The standard applies to:

- Apartments
- Villas
- Independent Houses
- Gated Communities

Future versions may include commercial and industrial properties.

---

# PHI Philosophy

A residential property should be treated as a living asset.

Just as people monitor their health using medical checkups and credit bureaus monitor financial health using credit scores, HomeSure AI continuously evaluates the health of a property.

The Property Health Index™ (PHI™) is designed to provide homeowners with a single, understandable measure of overall property health while remaining fully transparent and evidence-based.

The objective of PHI™ is not merely to score a property, but to help homeowners improve it over time.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Property Health Score must be:

- Objective
- Transparent
- Explainable
- Repeatable
- Data-driven
- Evidence-based

Every score produced by HomeSure AI should be supported by verifiable information.

# Property Health Pillars

The current version evaluates six major pillars.

## 1. Legal & Ownership

Purpose

Verify legal ownership and statutory approvals.

Examples

- Sale Deed
- Occupancy Certificate
- Khata / eKhata
- RERA Registration
- Approved Building Plan

Weight

25%

---

## 2. Construction Quality

Purpose

Evaluate structural quality and workmanship.

Examples

- Structural Cracks
- Water Leakage
- Dampness
- Flooring Quality
- Paint Quality
- Waterproofing

Weight

25%

---

## 3. Utilities & Infrastructure

Purpose

Evaluate operational readiness.

Examples

- BESCOM
- BWSSB
- STP
- WTP
- DG Backup
- Lifts
- Water Availability

Weight

15%

---

## 4. Safety & Compliance

Purpose

Evaluate statutory and resident safety.

Examples

- Fire NOC
- Emergency Exits
- Fire Fighting System
- CCTV
- Security
- Lightning Protection

Weight

15%

---

## 5. Community & Maintenance

Purpose

Evaluate long-term maintenance readiness.

Examples

- Association Formation
- Corpus Fund
- AMC
- Vendor Contracts
- Complaint Resolution

Weight

10%

---

## 6. Documentation

Purpose

Evaluate completeness of supporting documentation.

Examples

- Property Tax
- BESCOM Documents
- BWSSB Documents
- Warranty Documents
- Maintenance Manuals

Weight

10%

# Assessment Methodology

Every assessment question has:

- Unique Identifier
- Category
- Weight
- Mandatory Flag
- Required Evidence
- Applicable Property Type

Each response contributes proportionally to the category score.

# Confidence Score

Every PHI calculation includes a Confidence Score that reflects the completeness and reliability of the available information.

Confidence is influenced by:

- Verified documents
- Assessment completeness
- AI verification
- User confirmations
- Third-party data availability

Confidence should always be displayed alongside PHI.

Example:

PHI = 78

Confidence = 94%
