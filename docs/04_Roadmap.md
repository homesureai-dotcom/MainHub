# HomeSure AI Product Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Product Team  
**Last Updated:** August 2026

---

# Purpose

This roadmap defines the evolution of HomeSure AI from a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) into a comprehensive Property Health Intelligence Platform.

The roadmap provides direction while allowing flexibility as customer feedback and market needs evolve.

---

# Product Vision

HomeSure AI aims to become the trusted standard for evaluating, improving, and monitoring the health of residential properties.

Every feature added to the platform must support one or more of the following objectives:

- Increase transparency
- Reduce buyer risk
- Improve property health
- Build trust through evidence
- Simplify decision making

---

# Phase 1 – MVP (Version 1.0)

## Goal

Allow apartment buyers and homeowners to assess a property and receive an explainable Property Health Score.

### Features

### Property Management

- Add Property
- Property Profile
- Apartment Selection

---

### Assessment

- Property Health Assessment
- Category-wise Questions
- Progress Tracking
- Resume Assessment

---

### Assessment Engine

- HomeSure Property Health Standard (PHS)
- Weighted Scoring
- Category Results
- Property Health Grade

---

### Dashboard

- HomeSure Score
- Category Scores
- Assessment Summary
- Top Recommendations

---

### Recommendations

- Priority-based Actions
- Estimated Score Improvement
- Explainable Recommendations

---

### Property Health Certificate

- Download PDF
- Assessment Summary
- Category Breakdown
- Recommendations

---

### SureBot AI (Version 1)

- Explain assessment results
- Explain recommendations
- Answer assessment-related questions

---

## Success Criteria

Users can:

- Complete an assessment
- Understand their score
- Download a Property Health Certificate
- Know what actions to take next

# Product Principles

Every new feature must satisfy at least one of these principles:

- Improve Property Health
- Increase Trust
- Reduce Risk
- Simplify Decision Making
- Support Explainable AI
- Be Backed by Evidence

Features that do not support these principles should not be added to the roadmap.
